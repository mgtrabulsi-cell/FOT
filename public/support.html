Need help with GameWire? Email mgtrabulsi@gmail.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can. Please include your device model and a short description of the problem.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Choose "Continue as guest" to browse scores, stats, and news. An account lets you sync favorites and receive notifications.

How often do scores update? Live scoreboards refresh automatically about every 30 seconds while a game is in progress.

How do I turn notifications on or off? Open your Profile in the app, or change notification permissions in your device settings.

How do I delete my account? Open your Profile and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and associated data.
