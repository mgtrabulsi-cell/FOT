Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

GameWire ("we", "us") provides NFL scores, player statistics, news, and analysis at gamewire.us and in the GameWire app. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we store your email address, the username you choose, and a securely hashed password. You can use GameWire as a guest without an account.
  • Your preferences. Favorite teams and players, and which posts you have read, so they sync across your devices.
  • Push notification token. Only if you turn on notifications, we store a device token so we can send the alerts you chose. You can turn them off at any time in your profile or device settings.
  • Content you post. If you are an approved GameWire contributor, the posts and photos you publish are stored and shown publicly.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or payment information. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking, and we do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Information stored on your device

To load quickly and work offline, GameWire saves recent scores, statistics, team logos, and player photos on your device. This data contains public sports information and your preferences; it stays on your device and is removed when you delete your account or clear the app's data.

How we use information

We use your information only to provide GameWire: signing you in, syncing your favorites, showing unread updates, sending notifications you requested, and keeping the service secure.

Service providers

Accounts, preferences, and posts are stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Sports scores and statistics are loaded from ESPN's public data feeds; we do not send your personal information to ESPN. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from your Profile in the app. You can also email us at mgtrabulsi@gmail.com to request deletion.

Children

GameWire is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
